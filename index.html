A new way to move: don’t

Every game you have ever played gives you a way to move — thrust, steering, a jump button. Motion Thief takes all of that away. Nothing in this world can change its own motion. But motion can be exchanged, and you are the only one who can exchange it.

STEP 01

Select a body

Tap your orb, a comet, or a resting rock. Its arrow shows exactly where its motion will take it — a dotted line traces its path.

STEP 02

Tap a second body

The two bodies instantly trade velocities — speed and direction, whole. Steal a comet’s speed. Dump your own speed into a rock to brake dead. Aim a comet at a mine by giving it your heading.

STEP 03

Ride it to the portal

Swaps are limited, so spend them like currency. Drift, coast and bounce — walls and collisions are perfectly elastic, so stolen motion never fades.

Field guide

  • Your orb. Helpless on its own — no engine at all. If it touches a mine, it shatters.
  • Comets & rocks. Drifting bodies, all swappable. Heavier ones shove lighter ones around in collisions.
  • The portal. Drift your orb into the ring to clear the level. In the finale, the portal drifts too.
  • Mines. Fatal to your orb — but a comet sent into one destroys them both. Clear your own road.
  • Charge cells. Drift through one to gain +2 swaps. Only your orb can collect them.
  • Motion arrows. Every body wears its velocity as an arrow — length is speed. Read the field before you spend a swap.
