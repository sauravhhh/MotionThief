Learn it in five seconds

No steering, no buttons to memorise, nothing to collect or upgrade. Your finger makes ripples, ripples push things — like tapping the surface of a pond. If you can tap, you can play.

1

Tap anywhere

A ripple spreads out from your fingertip. Anything inside it gets a gentle push, away from where you tapped.

2

Aim with position

Want a firefly to go right? Tap to its left. Want it to slow down? Tap in front of it. Where you tap is the steering.

3

Guide them home

Push each firefly into the glowing green ring before time runs out. Every catch lights the garden up and moves the ring somewhere new.

Who’s who in the garden

  • Fireflies. The golden lights. They drift and daydream — nudge them home.
  • The home ring. Green and glowing. A firefly that drifts inside it is safely home.
  • Thorn puffs. Prickly red drifters, from level 3. Ripples push them too — keep them out of the ring, or you lose 5 seconds.
  • Your ripple. The only tool you have. Small taps, close to a firefly, push hardest.
